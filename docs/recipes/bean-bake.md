# Bean Bake

A sweet and savory baked bean dish with bacon, ground beef, onions, and a tangy brown sugar-ketchup sauce.

<img src="/assets/recipes/bean-bake.jpg" class="recipe-image">

> :octicons-stopwatch-16: **Prep** 15 min  
> :octicons-stopwatch-16: **Cook** 40 min  
> :octicons-stopwatch-16: **Total** 55 min  
> :octicons-people-16: **Serves** 8

## Ingredients

- **½ lb** bacon, chopped
- **1 lb** ground beef
- **1 cup** onion, chopped
- **½ cup** brown sugar
- **½ cup** ketchup
- **½ tsp** salt
- **1 tsp** mustard
- **2 tsp** vinegar
- Lima beans
- Kidney beans
- Pork and beans

## Instructions

1. **Brown the meat and onion.** In a large skillet, cook the bacon, onion, and ground beef until the meat is browned and the onion is softened. Drain excess grease if needed.
2. **Add the sauce ingredients.** Stir in the salt, brown sugar, vinegar, mustard, and ketchup. Mix until well combined.
3. **Add the beans.** Stir in the lima beans, kidney beans, and pork and beans.
4. **Bake.** Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and bake at **350°F (175°C) for 40 minutes**, until hot and bubbling.
